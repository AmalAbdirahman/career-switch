What we built for the demo

Due to time constraints, we implemented the frontend only, using predefined data. It still brings our idea to life: a supportive, compassionate, community-based app for people taking their first steps towards a new career.

Challenges and what is next

Challenges

  • Time constraints: we focused on the frontend so we could show the full user experience.
  • Predefined data: without a live backend, accounts, real-time community posts and saved progress are not working yet.

Next steps

  • Connect the backend: Firebase and Google Cloud Platform for live data.
  • AI chatbot: deeper, real-time guidance when users need it.
  • Machine learning: insights from user data to improve learning paths and the app itself.

Monetisation and revenue

Memberships fund us from launch. Partnerships scale us long term.

1. Membership: Pathfinder Plus

£9.99/month

A free core community drives growth. Users who want more personal support upgrade to get:

  • ✓Priority access to leading experts in your target industry
  • ✓Members-only events and sessions
  • ✓Curated learning pathways
  • ✓In-person CV and interview 1-to-1s
  • ✓Personalised recommendations
Target conversion: 5% of active users

2. Partnerships

About £15,000 per year

Per partner. Universities, job centres and government employment programmes license CareerSwitch to support their students and job seekers.

One partnership onboards users in bulk, which lowers acquisition costs and supports our goal of reducing job anxiety and unemployment.

Illustrative scale: 100,000 active users at 5% conversion is 5,000 members, about £50,000 a month. Ten partners is £150,000 a year.
